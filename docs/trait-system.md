# Trait System

## Overview

The Trait System replaces traditional character classes with a modular progression model where a character's identity is defined by the philosophies they embrace, the specializations they pursue, and the combinations they create.

Rather than selecting a fixed class such as Warrior, Mage or Assassin, every player begins their journey as an **Adventurer**. As they grow stronger, they discover the Paths that define their combat philosophy. Later in the game, they may either deepen their mastery of a single Path or awaken a second Path to create a hybrid playstyle.

The design goals of the system are:

- Replace rigid classes with player-driven character identity.
- Encourage experimentation through hybrid builds.
- Allow meaningful long-term progression.
- Keep the system easy to understand while offering high build diversity.

---

# Progression

Every character begins as an **Adventurer**.

Progression follows four major milestones:

```text
Level 1
Choose your First Path

↓

Level 30
Choose your First Specialization

↓

Level 50
Choose one:

• Awaken a Second Path
OR
• Deepen your Current Path

↓

Level 70

If you awakened a Second Path:
    Choose its Specialization.

If you remained Pure:
    Unlock the Master Specialization for your original Path.

↓

Level 99
Master your build.
```

This progression ensures that every major milestone introduces a meaningful decision rather than simply increasing numerical power.

# Paths

## "Which force guides your character?"

Paths represent the fundamental philosophies of the world rather than combat roles.

Each Path defines how a character approaches combat.

## Instinct

**Fantasy**

Speed, precision, survival and adaptation.

**Gameplay Identity**

- Burst damage
- Critical strikes
- Mobility
- Precision
- Evasion

---

## Might

**Fantasy**

Strength, resilience and unwavering determination.

**Gameplay Identity**

- High durability
- Sustained melee combat
- Counter attacks
- Armor
- Frontline combat

---

## Wisdom

**Fantasy**

Knowledge, arcane mastery and tactical superiority.

**Gameplay Identity**

- Spell power
- Cooldown management
- Crowd Control
- Resource manipulation
- Tactical gameplay

---

## Harmony

**Fantasy**

Life, balance and coexistence with nature.

**Gameplay Identity**

- Healing
- Summons
- Buffs
- Protection
- Adaptability

---

# Path Structure

Each Path contains:

- 12 Talent Nodes
- Up to 5 ranks per Talent
- 60 available Talent Ranks
- 4 Active Skills
- Multiple Keystone Talents

Each Path should provide enough options that no player can fully complete it.

# Specializations

## "How do you choose to express your Path?"

Specializations represent a refined expression of a Path.

Unlike traditional classes, they are not permanent identities. They simply define the direction a Path evolves.

# Specialization Structure

Each Specialization contains:

- 8 Talent Nodes
- Up to 5 ranks per Talent
- 40 available Talent Ranks
- 4 Active Skills
- Unique Keystone Talents

Specializations expand a Path instead of replacing it.

---

# Talent Trees

Talent Trees are divided into three categories.

## Passive Talents

Small, repeatable improvements.

Examples:

- Critical Chance
- Maximum Health
- Spell Power
- Summon Damage

---

## Utility Talents

Gameplay modifications.

Examples:

- Reduced Cooldowns
- Increased Range
- Faster Resource Generation
- Longer Crowd Control Duration

---

## Keystone Talents

Powerful build-defining talents.

Only a few Keystones can be selected.

Examples:

- Critical strikes refresh Dash.
- Healing also grants temporary shields.
- Summons inherit a percentage of your attributes.
- Counter attacks always trigger after blocking.

Keystones should fundamentally change gameplay instead of simply increasing damage.

---

# Pure vs Hybrid Builds

At Level 50 every player chooses between two progression paths.

## Hybrid Build

Awaken a second Path.

Example:

```
Instinct

↓

Predator

↓

Harmony

↓

Oracle
```

Hybrid builds gain access to two different philosophies, allowing greater flexibility and unique combinations.

---

## Pure Build

Remain loyal to your original Path.

Example:

```
Instinct

↓

Predator

↓

Master Instinct

↓

Master Predator
```

Pure builds unlock exclusive talents and stronger Path-specific bonuses that are unavailable to hybrids.

Both approaches should be equally viable.

---

# Synergy System

Hybrid characters unlock Synergy Talents based on the combination of their Paths.

Synergy Talents represent interactions between two philosophies rather than belonging to either Path individually.

Examples:

## Instinct + Harmony

Nature Predator

Examples:

- Bleeding enemies feed nearby summons.
- Healing increases Critical Chance.
- Dodging empowers companion attacks.

---

## Might + Wisdom

Battle Scholar

Examples:

- Blocking reduces spell cooldowns.
- Casting spells increases Armor.
- Heavy attacks generate Mana.

---

## Wisdom + Harmony

Spirit Sage

Examples:

- Summons cast support abilities.
- Healing spreads between allies.
- Buffs gain increased duration.

---

## Instinct + Might

Relentless Hunter

Examples:

- Critical strikes grant temporary Armor.
- Charge attacks reset Dash.
- Executions restore Health.

The exact Synergy Talents will evolve during development, but every hybrid combination should feel distinct.

# Skills

Each Path provides:

- 4 Active Skills

Each Specialization provides:

- 4 Active Skills

A fully progressed character has access to:

- 16 Active Skills

However, only **6 Skills may be equipped simultaneously**.

This makes skill selection one of the primary build decisions.

---

# Skill Progression

Each equipped skill contains:

- Active Skill
  - Levels 1–10

- Skill Mastery
  - 5 Levels

The final Mastery Rank should modify gameplay instead of simply increasing numerical values.

Examples:

- Adds an Execute mechanic.
- Converts damage type.
- Applies a new debuff.
- Allows the skill to chain between enemies.
- Causes healing to affect summons.

---

# Skill Unlock Requirements

| Skill Rank | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| Levels 1–3 | Immediately available |
| Levels 4–5 | Character Level 20 |
| Levels 6–7 | Character Level 40 |
| Levels 8–9 | Character Level 60 |
| Level 10 | Character Level 80 |
| Mastery | Character Level 90 |

---

# Character Progression

By Level 99 a character has access to:

## Paths

- 2 Paths (or 1 Master Path)
- 2 Specializations

## Talents

- 40 Talent Nodes
- 200 Available Talent Ranks
- 99 Talent Points

## Skills

- 16 Available Active Skills
- 6 Equipped Skill Slots
- 99 Skill Points

No character can unlock every Talent, ensuring meaningful specialization while allowing complete mastery of their chosen skill loadout.

---

# Design Principles

The Trait System is built around the following principles:

- Every build should feel unique.
- Identity is created through player choices rather than predefined classes.
- Hybrid builds should offer flexibility.
- Pure builds should offer deeper specialization.
- Powerful effects should come from meaningful combinations instead of raw numbers.
- Every Path should remain valuable throughout the entire progression.
- Character progression should be defined by interesting decisions, not mandatory upgrades.
